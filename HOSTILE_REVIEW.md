# Hostile pre-submission review

Review completed before mechanical QC from cardiac-anaesthesia, trial-methodology, biostatistics, pharmacology/ethics, reproducibility, and skeptical-ACA-editor perspectives.

## Highest priority—required before formal submission

### ACA does not list a protocol article type

- **Lethality:** Desk-reject risk: high.
- **Correction effect:** Very high.
- **Feasibility:** Immediate.
- **Action:** Do not upload as an Original Article without editorial direction. The package now contains a presubmission inquiry and repeatedly labels article-type eligibility unresolved.
- **Status:** Corrected in package; external editorial decision remains required.

### Mandatory trial metadata are not yet real

- **Lethality:** Ethical/regulatory invalidity if fabricated: critical.
- **Correction effect:** Essential.
- **Feasibility:** Requires sponsor/investigator action.
- **Action:** Keep registry, ethics, sponsor, funding, site, recruitment, compensation, and final author-contribution fields explicitly pending. Do not enrol or formally submit until completed.
- **Status:** Not fabricated; documented as a release blocker.

### FPA safety and legal status

- **Lethality:** Major ethical/regulatory concern.
- **Correction effect:** Essential.
- **Feasibility:** Requires Japanese sponsor/regulatory confirmation.
- **Action:** State that prophylaxis is off-label/investigational; exclude planned CABG conservatively; operationalise organ-dysfunction thresholds against the current Japanese label; require SAE/SUSAR procedures and DSMB review.
- **Status:** Corrected; final regulatory classification remains external.

## High priority

### Primary endpoint is not a validated universal definition of shock

- **Lethality:** Major-revision risk: high.
- **Correction effect:** High.
- **Feasibility:** Completed.
- **Action:** Define it as a protocol-specific “clinically important peri-protamine hypotension” endpoint, not shock; require a sustained artefact-free 60-second epoch; add MAP <65 mmHg, hypotension burden, rescue, and continuous outcomes.
- **Status:** Corrected.

### Rescue treatment can obscure the haemodynamic effect

- **Lethality:** High analytical-bias risk.
- **Correction effect:** High.
- **Feasibility:** Completed.
- **Action:** Use a treatment-policy primary estimand, retain post-rescue MAP, collect exact intervention timestamps, and prespecify a rescue composite and pre-rescue supportive summaries.
- **Status:** Corrected.

### Indirect and uncertain sample-size assumptions

- **Lethality:** Recruitment failure or underpowering risk: high.
- **Correction effect:** High.
- **Feasibility:** Completed.
- **Action:** Do not use the retrospective zero-event estimate as truth. Use an external 12.9% planning anchor, conservative 6.0% treatment risk, 90% power, 5% non-evaluable allowance, N=800, sensitivity scenarios, and blinded pooled-rate reassessment without treatment-effect access.
- **Status:** Corrected; feasibility requires confirmation by participating sites.

### Protamine heterogeneity may dominate the endpoint

- **Lethality:** Major confounding/precision concern.
- **Correction effect:** High.
- **Feasibility:** Completed at protocol level.
- **Action:** Prefer residual-heparin titration, impose local-label infusion limits and a hard guideline ceiling, record all dose/rate/ACT details, and adjust for site through randomisation and analysis.
- **Status:** Corrected; site implementation manuals remain to be finalised.

## Medium priority

### Stratification could create sparse cells

- **Lethality:** Moderate.
- **Correction effect:** Moderate.
- **Feasibility:** Completed.
- **Action:** Use central variable-block allocation and only site plus prior protamine/protamine-insulin exposure. Permit incomplete final blocks and use sparse-event methods.
- **Status:** Corrected.

### The CABG exclusion narrows generalisability

- **Lethality:** Moderate.
- **Correction effect:** Moderate.
- **Feasibility:** Completed.
- **Action:** Explain this as a conservative response to international systemic-NSAID warnings, not as proof of a Japanese FPA-specific CABG prohibition.
- **Status:** Corrected.

### “Severe cardiac disease” would be unusably vague in a cardiac-surgery trial

- **Lethality:** Moderate-to-high eligibility ambiguity.
- **Correction effect:** High.
- **Feasibility:** Completed.
- **Action:** Replace the blanket term with decompensated heart failure/cardiogenic shock and require final numeric/clinical thresholds against the current label.
- **Status:** Corrected.

### Figure handling could conflict with LWW submission practice

- **Lethality:** Technical-return risk.
- **Correction effect:** Moderate.
- **Feasibility:** Completed.
- **Action:** The blinded submission manuscript contains callouts and end legends but no embedded figures; individual TIFF/PNG files are supplied. A separate inline-figures manuscript and editable PPTX are provided for review/reference.
- **Status:** Corrected.

It is not ready for participant enrolment or formal ACA submission until external metadata, approvals, registration, sponsor decisions, and ACA article-type eligibility are confirmed. No prospective efficacy claim or invented clinical result was identified.
